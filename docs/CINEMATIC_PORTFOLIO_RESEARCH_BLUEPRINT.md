## 1. Executive Vision & Core Positioning

### The Creative Tension to Resolve
Most developer portfolios fail in one of two opposite ways:
1. **The "Flashy Tech Demo" Trap:** Heavy dark-mode WebGL scenes with slow loaders, chaotic neon colors, cryptic navigation, and zero business impact — impressing junior developers for 10 seconds while frustrating CTOs, VPs of Engineering, and luxury clients.
2. **The "Sterile Resume" Trap:** Flat monochrome grids that list frameworks without proving taste, interactive mastery, systems thinking, or product leadership.

### The Winning Aesthetic Formula: **"Sunlit Kinetic Atelier"**
Combine three elite design disciplines into one unmistakable signature:
- **Cinema Direction** (Cartier *365 / The Fabulous Journey*, Lusion *Oryzo*, Unseen Studio): Continuous camera choreography, act-based pacing, purposeful framing, and emotional Peaks and Ends.
- **Precision SaaS Engineering** (Stripe, Linear, Vercel, Apple): Razor-sharp Swiss grids, live architectural clarity, sub-400ms tactile responsiveness, quantified engineering outcomes, and effortless scannability.
- **Architectural Luxury in Light Mode** (Porcelain galleries, Dieter Rams / Braun tactile hardware, Porsche Taycan chalk-ceramic + luminous light blades): Warm mineral alabaster surfaces where **Electric Neon Teal** and **Electric Neon Orange** behave like physical light refracted through optical glass — never like flat cyberpunk stickers.

### Who You Are Designing For (The 6-Second Rule)
Hiring managers, founders, and enterprise clients spend **6 to 8 seconds** on an initial portfolio scan, and **94% of first impressions are design-related**. Within **3 seconds** of landing on any screen, a visitor must immediately grasp:
1. **Identity & Seniority:** *Senior Software Engineer & Creative Web Architect* who ships mission-critical systems and flagship web products.
2. **Proof of Mastery:** The site *itself* is a living, buttery-smooth, responsive engineering artifact.
3. **Quantified Impact:** Real numbers (latency cut, revenue lift, uptime, scale, Lighthouse/INP craft) paired with narrative depth via progressive disclosure.

Motivation & Emotion** | **Peak-End Rule (#50)** | Engineer two unforgettable highs: **The Peak** (exploding an interactive 3D architecture model inside a flagship case study) and **The End** (a serene, tactile luxury concierge finale & instant confirmation ritual). | Ending a gorgeous portfolio with a plain generic `mailto:` footer. |

Product Strategy** | **Progressive Disclosure (#59) & Tesler's Law (#60)** | Show crisp executive story first; let engineers toggle **"Inspect Architecture / Specs"** overlays to reveal system diagrams, code snippets, and benchmarks on demand. ## 3. Cinematic Storytelling Architecture (Every Section, Screen & Page)

Rather than treating the portfolio as stacked marketing bands, direct it like a **five-act feature film** with seamless **inter-page camera continuity**.

### Persistent Theatrical Frame (Always Present, Never Cluttered)
- **Top Micro-Header (Glass Hairline Bar):** Monogram logo (left), 4 concise nav items (center), live local availability pill with pulsing neon-teal status dot + subtle Sound Toggle + `TV/Cinema Mode` toggle (right).
- **Chapter Timecode Rail (Side / Bottom depending on device):** Shows current narrative chapter (`ACT I — THESIS · 01/05`), scroll scrub indicator in hairline teal with a neon-orange playhead tick, and direct jump anchors.
- **Persistent Shared 3D Canvas (`<Canvas>` mounted in root layout):** Never unmounts on client-side route navigation. Moving from `/` → `/work/lumina-cloud` smoothly glides the 3D camera into that project's bespoke hero artifact instead of flashing white!

### Home Page (`/`): The 5-Act Narrative Arc

#### Prologue — *The Aperture Open* (0.0s – 0.8s, Non-Blocking)
- **Visual:** Warm porcelain screen opens via a crisp architectural aperture reveal (no fake waiting). Real-time shader warm-up displays a 2-line monospace status tick only if load exceeds 300ms (*Doherty Threshold + honest Labor Illusion*).
- **Emotional Goal:** Immediate calm authority — like stepping into an architectural gallery bathed in morning daylight.

#### Act I — *The Thesis (Hero Establishing Shot)*
- **Layout:** Asymmetric 12-column editorial split (Desktop/Laptop) or vertically staged hero (Mobile).
- **Left/Foreground:** Sculptural editorial headline combining authority + craft (e.g., *"Engineering Software That Feels Like Cinema"*), concise senior-level positioning subcopy, primary **Neon-Orange Tactile CTA** (`Explore Selected Works`), secondary **Teal Hairline CTA** (`Book a Technical Call`), and 3 anchor telemetry badges (*Anchoring Effect*: e.g., `8+ Years Production`, `99.98% Uptime Systems`, `Awwwards-Grade Frontend`).
- **Right/Stage:** Interactive 3D **"Architectural Prism Engine"** floating in sunlit ceramic space, gently breathing on idle and magnetically tilting toward the cursor/touch.
- **Scroll Beat:** As you begin scrolling, the headline gracefully tracks upward while the 3D Prism smoothly rotates and begins separating its translucent layers — pulling you seamlessly into Act II.

#### Act II — *The Dual Craft (Engineering Philosophy & Capabilities — Sticky Multi-Slide Stage)*
- **Narrative Purpose:** Prove you are *both* a **deep Senior Software Engineer** (distributed systems, APIs, performance, security, architecture) *and* a **master Website Developer** (WebGL, design systems, accessibility, fluid UX).
- **Cinematic Execution (Sticky Multi-Slide Full-Screen Takeover):** On desktop/laptop, the viewport **pins smoothly in place** while scrolling transitions sequentially through **4 full-viewport architectural slides** (via crisp curtain wipes + subtle depth recession) as the 3D Prism explodes into 4 interactive physical strata:
  1. **Slide 01 — Foundation (Distributed Backend & Cloud Architecture)** — Deep Teal core glow
  2. **Slide 02 — Engine (Type-Safe Systems, State & Real-Time Data)** — Electric Teal conduits
  3. **Slide 03 — Surface (Design Systems, Fluid Layouts & Micro-Physics)** — Frosted Ceramic glass
  4. **Slide 04 — Cinema (3D, Shaders, Motion & Emotional Storytelling)** — Electric Neon Orange focal prism
- **Exit Continuity:** Upon completing Slide 04, the stage smoothly unpins and hands momentum directly into Act III. A subtle slide indicator (`02 / 04`) and clickable jump dots keep the visitor fully oriented.
- **Interactive Storytelling:** Clicking/focusing any stratum highlights its connected data packets in 3D and reveals concrete real-world engineering principles + stack proof (*Progressive Disclosure*).

#### Act III — *The Feature Films (Selected Case Studies Showcase — Vertical-to-Horizontal Pinned Reel)*
- **Narrative Purpose:** Present **3 to 5 flagship projects** like premiered feature films — each with its own visual mood, quantified metric headline, architectural role, and bespoke 3D artifact.
- **Cinematic Execution (Vertical-to-Horizontal Scroll Reel):**
  - **Desktop/Laptop:** As Act III reaches the top of the viewport, vertical scroll seamlessly pivots into a **wide horizontal cinemascope film strip**. Each scroll increment glides horizontally across full-height theatrical project panels (`1:1` scroll-to-x sync with `ease: "none"`), while nested `containerAnimation` triggers choreograph per-card parallax, headline reveals, and synchronized 3D artifact morphs (shifting ambient lighting between Teal-dominant for infrastructure/SaaS and Orange-dominant for product/conversion). Once the final flagship panel finishes (plus an `"Explore Full Archive →"` end-cap), scrolling **seamlessly resumes vertical movement** into Act IV.
  - **Visual Peek & Control:** The next project panel intentionally **bleeds into the right edge** (*Law of Continuity*), paired with a live horizontal scrubber bar, prev/next buttons, and a `"Skip to Lab ↓"` shortcut so visitors never feel trapped.
  - Each panel includes a **3-second scannable dossier**: Film Number (`01`), Project Title, One-Line Transformation Statement, **Headline Metric** (`-62% Checkout Latency · +38% Conversion`), Role + Year, and a magnetic `Enter Case Study →` trigger.

#### Act IV — *The Living Atelier (Interactive Engineering Lab & Technical Proof)*
- **Narrative Purpose:** Show, don't just tell. Senior engineers and founders love tangible proof of systems + UI mastery.
- **Cinematic Execution:** A sleek SaaS-style interactive Bento Workbench embedded in porcelain glass cards:
  - **Live 3D Architecture / Performance Simulator:** Let visitors drag a "Traffic / Concurrency" slider or toggle "Edge Caching / Spatial Rendering" and watch the live 3D data topology and real-time latency gauge react in neon teal & orange (*Flow + IKEA Effect*).
  - **Live Design-Engineering Micro-Spec:** Toggle an *"X-Ray Grid & Telemetry Mode"* directly on the portfolio itself to inspect the live CSS grid, 60fps frame graph, token architecture, and responsive container boundaries! (Huge differentiator for senior engineers!)

#### Act V — *The Climax & Concierge Finale (Testimonials, Recognition & Commission)*
- **Narrative Purpose:** Close with trust, authority, and an effortless high-luxury conversion moment (*Peak-End Rule*).
- **Cinematic Execution:**
  - **Editorial Endorsements:** Restrained high-contrast pull quotes from Engineering Leaders / Founders with clean hairline rules and portrait treatments.
  - **The Concierge Finale (Contact Section):** The 3D camera glides into a serene, minimalist crystalline beacon bathed in warm sunlit light. Visitors can choose one of **3 low-friction paths** (*Hick's Law*):
    1. *Hire for Senior / Staff Engineering Role* (Instant resume/dossier download + direct calendar)
    2. *Commission a Flagship Web / SaaS Product* (Interactive 3-step luxury brief builder with instant scope/timeline feedback)
    3. *Quick Direct Note* (One-click copy email with tactile confirmation pulse)
  - **The End Ritual:** Submitting a message triggers a crisp, celebratory neon-orange-to-teal light wave across the 3D beacon and an executive receipt card confirming exact response time.

## 4. Light-Mode Luxury Color System: Mastering Neon Orange & Neon Teal

### Why Neon on Light Mode Usually Fails — And How to Make It Look Like a $100K Luxury Brand
Placing raw neon `#FF6600` or `#00FFE5` text on pure `#FFFFFF` causes **optical vibration (chromostereopsis)**, fails WCAG contrast (`#00E5CC` on `#FFFFFF` is only `1.6:1`!), and feels cheap/gaming-themed rather than luxury.

### The Breakthrough Strategy: **Warm Architectural Mineral Canvas + Dual-Tier Optical Neon**
1. **Warm Porcelain Base (Never Sterile `#FFFFFF`):** A warm, gallery-paper alabaster canvas softens optical glare and makes luminous accents feel like **sunlight hitting dichroic glass and brushed ceramic**.
2. **Split Every Accent Into Two Strict Functional Tiers:**
   - **Optical / Emissive Tier (P3 Wide-Gamut Neon):** Used *exclusively* for 3D light emission, caustic edge reflections, hairline animated borders, glow pulses, hover auras, and high-contrast dark-glass pills.
   - **Accessible Ink / Fill Tier (WCAG 2.2 AA/AAA):** Used for all readable text, icons, buttons, focus rings, and interactive strokes on light surfaces.

### Complete Design Token Palette (With Verified WCAG 2.2 Contrast Guidance)

| Role | Token Name | Hex (sRGB Fallback) | Wide-Gamut `oklch()` | Usage Rule & Contrast |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Canvas** | `--bg-alabaster` | `#F7F5F0` | `oklch(0.97 0.008 85)` | Main page ground (75% of viewport); warm museum stone feel |
| **Elevated Surface** | `--surface-porcelain` | `#FFFFFF` | `oklch(1.00 0.000 0)` | Bento cards, frosted glass base, floating docks |

### The 75 / 15 / 7 / 3 Luxury Rationing Formula
- **75% Warm Alabaster & Porcelain:** Gives the luxury sense of space, silence, and confidence.
- **15% Deep Obsidian Ink & Mineral Hairlines:** Delivers Swiss editorial authority and instant scannability.
- **7% Structural Teal (`--teal-deep` + `--neon-teal`):** Represents engineering calm, reliability, architecture, and live system flow.
- **3% Electric Orange (`--neon-orange` + `--orange-lacquer`):** Strictly rationed via the **Von Restorff Effect** for primary conversion moments, active narrative highlights, and kinetic focal accents.

### Secret Optical Technique: **"Light-Mode Dichroic Glow"**
Instead of dark-mode blurred drop shadows (which turn muddy brown on light backgrounds), create a true luxury neon sensation in light mode via:
1. **Colored Specular Border Illumination:** A `1px` crisp gradient border (`linear-gradient(135deg, #00E5CC, transparent 45%, #FF5A1F)`) that tracks cursor angle on frosted porcelain cards.
2. **Ambient Colored Bounce Shadow:** Ultra-diffuse, low-opacity tinted elevation (`0 20px 50px -15px rgba(0, 229, 204, 0.16), 0 8px 24px -10px rgba(255, 90, 31, 0.14)`) simulating neon light bouncing off matte white stone.
3. **Selective Obsidian Micro-Islands:** Small high-contrast dark obsidian pills (e.g., live FPS/status badge, interactive code inspector, floating command bar `⌘K`) where pure neon teal and neon orange shine like jewel instruments against the sunlit porcelain canvas.

## 5. Luxury Design Language: Typography, Materiality & Invisible Craft

Luxury is felt in the **micro-details most visitors cannot consciously name** (*Aesthetic-Usability Effect*).

### Typography Trio (Editorial Haute Couture + Precision Engineering)
1. **Display / Cinematic Headlines:** A high-craft editorial neo-grotesk or refined serif-grotesk pairing:
   - *Option A (Architectural Luxury SaaS — Recommended):* **Instrument Serif** (select italic accents for emotional verbs like *Cinema*, *Craft*, *Precision*) paired with **Satoshi** or **Neue Haas Grotesk / Plus Jakarta Sans** (tight tracking `-0.03em`, line-height `1.02–1.08`).
   - *Option B (Pure Swiss Avant-Garde):* **General Sans / Switzer** Bold (`-0.035em` tracking) with extreme scale contrast.
2. **Body / Narrative Copy:** **Inter** or **Plus Jakarta Sans** (`400/500`, `line-height: 1.6`, strictly capped at **`60–72ch`** max measure for effortless reading).
3. **Technical / Film Metadata:** **JetBrains Mono** or **IBM Plex Mono** (`11px–13px`, uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.12em`) for film timecodes (`ACT 02 · 00:42`), performance specs (`LCP 1.1s · 60 FPS`), coordinates, and architectural specs.

### Tactile Surface Materiality
- **Porcelain Paper Grain:** A subtle SVG fractal-noise overlay fixed at `opacity: 0.022` (`pointer-events: none`) eliminates the flat "plastic CSS" look and makes light-mode gradients feel printed on archival cotton stock.
- **Liquid Frosted Acrylic (Glassmorphism Done Right):**
  ```css
  background: linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.78), rgba(247,245,240,0.62));
  backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(165%);
  border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85);
  box-shadow:
    inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9),
    0 12px 32px -12px rgba(14, 23, 26, 0.08);
  ```
- **Precision Swiss Hairlines:** Use `1px` crisp structural grid rules with tiny geometric intersection ticks (`+`) at major section corners — evoking architectural blueprints and Swiss watchmaking.
- **Optional Tactile Foley Audio (Muted by Default):** A whisper-quiet Web Audio synthesizer (subtle mechanical detent click on chapter snap, airy harmonic shimmer on CTA hover) toggled cleanly in the header — never auto-playing!

## 6. Living Motion System: Clear, Smooth, Interactive & Alive

To make animations feel **alive** rather than robotic CSS slides, separate motion into **two cooperating systems** and tune them using **Disney's 12 Principles of Animation** + physical spring mathematics.

### Architecture: The Dual-Engine Motion Rule
1. **The Cinematic Director (`Lenis` + `GSAP ScrollTrigger`):**
   - Lenis provides weighted, liquid scroll momentum synchronized directly to `gsap.ticker`.
   - GSAP ScrollTrigger writes normalized scroll progress (`0 → 1`) and velocity into a plain mutable ref object (zero React re-renders during scroll!).
   - Drives reversible chapter timelines, 3D camera splines, editorial masked text reveals (`clip-path: inset(0 0 100% 0)` → `0`), and pinned case-study reveals.
2. **The Living Actor (`R3F useFrame` + `Framer Motion Springs`):**
   - **Never freezes when the user stops scrolling!**
   - Subtle ambient life runs continuously: slow organic breathing on the 3D glass prism, drifting caustic light rays, gentle orbital telemetry pulses, and real-time pointer/gyroscope parallax with velocity damping (`THREE.MathUtils.damp`).

### Signature "Alive" Interaction Vocabulary

| Interaction | Disney Principle Applied | Physics / Timing Spec | Visual Feel |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Magnetic Luxury Buttons** | Anticipation + Follow-Through | Spring `stiffness: 320, damping: 22`; inner label shifts slightly further than container (`1.3×` parallax) | Button leans subtly toward cursor before click, compresses (`scale: 0.97`) on press, releases with crisp neon-orange edge sweep |
| **Editorial Headline Reveal** | Staging + Slow In/Slow Out | Masked line-by-line rise (`y: 110% → 0%`), `duration: 0.75s`, `ease: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, `stagger: 0.07s` | Feels like precision cinema credits rising from behind an invisible mask |
| **Card Tilt & Specular Sheen** | Solid Drawing + Secondary Action | Real-time pointer tracking mapped to `±3deg` subtle 3D tilt + moving dichroic teal/orange specular highlight | Surface catches light like real beveled optical glass |
| **Scroll-Velocity Skew / Stretch** | Squash & Stretch (Restrained) | Lenis velocity softly modulates 3D particle drift and hairline progress tension (clamped tiny so text never blurs!) | Scrolling feels physically connected to the environment |
| **Chapter / Page Transitions** | Continuity + Common Fate | Persistent 3D camera eases along Catmull-Rom spline (`0.65s`) while outgoing DOM cross-dissolves upward and incoming rises in unison | Zero harsh cuts; moving across pages feels like one continuous Steadicam shot |
| **Custom Adaptive Cursor (Desktop)** | Affordances & Signifiers | Subtle translucent ring + center dot (`lerp 0.16`) that morphs into clean pill label (`VIEW FILM`, `DRAG 3D`, `INSPECT`) over interactive surfaces | Never obstructs text; disabled automatically on touch & TV inputs |

### Non-Negotiable Motion Guardrails
- **GPU-Composited Only:** Strictly animate `transform`, `opacity`, `clip-path`, and WebGL shader uniforms. Zero layout-thrashing animations (`width`, `height`, `top`, `left`).
- **Luxury Weight (No Toy Bounce):** High-end brands avoid cartoonish rubbery oscillation. Prefer critically damped or minimally overshooting curves (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` or springs with damping ratio `0.82–0.95`).

## 7. Cinematic Scroll Choreography: Vertical-to-Horizontal Reels & Sticky Multi-Slide Stages

Selective **vertical-to-horizontal scroll reels** and **sticky pinned multi-slide full-screen sequences** are two of the most recognizable signatures of Awwwards Site of the Month / luxury product launches (Apple, Porsche, Cartier, Active Theory). Used with discipline, they transform passive reading into **directed cinema**; used carelessly, they cause scroll-trapping, iOS Safari layout glitches, and accessibility failures.

### Strategic Rule: **Rhythm & Contrast (Never Back-to-Back Fatigue)**
Use pinned choreography **sparingly** — ideally **at most 2 pinned sequences per long page** (e.g., *Act II Sticky Multi-Slide* and *Act III Horizontal Film Reel*), buffered by breathable standard vertical sections (Act I Hero, Act IV Interactive Workbench, Act V Concierge Finale). This contrast makes every pinned transition feel intentional and theatrical rather than exhausting.

### Pattern A: Vertical-to-Horizontal Pinned Scroll Reel
> *Behavior:* The user scrolls vertically down the page → the section locks cleanly into the viewport → continued vertical scrolling slides a multi-panel horizontal gallery smoothly sideways → upon reaching the final panel, the section releases and normal vertical scrolling continues effortlessly.

#### Where It Shines in This Portfolio
- **Act III (Selected Flagship Case Studies):** 3–5 widescreen cinematic project panels + a final `"View Full Archive"` end-cap.
- **`/about` (Career & Architecture Timeline):** Horizontal era progression showing seniority growth from engineer → tech lead → systems architect.
- **`/work/[slug]` (Evolution Strip):** Optional mid-article horizontal storyboard showing *Sketch → Wireframe → Architecture → Final Shipped Build*.

#### Production-Grade GSAP + Lenis Engineering Rules
1. **Pin the Wrapper, Translate the Child Track:** Never animate the pinned element itself horizontally — `ScrollTrigger` uses transforms/fixed positioning to pin the outer container, so always translate `.horizontal-track` inside a pinned `.horizontal-stage` wrapper.
2. **Mandatory `ease: "none"` on the Outer Track Tween:** Any easing curve on the main horizontal translation warps the 1:1 relationship between scroll position and horizontal movement, making the reel feel rubbery and out of sync with the user's fingers/trackpad. Keep `ease: "none"` on the outer track and apply rich luxury easing exclusively to **nested child reveals**.
3. **Dynamic Resize Safety (`invalidateOnRefresh: true`):** Express `x` and `end` as functions (`() => -(track.scrollWidth - window.innerWidth)`) so resizing between laptop, desktop, and ultrawide recalculates exact scroll distance cleanly without overshoot or blank tails.
4. **Nested Per-Panel Choreography via `containerAnimation`:** Pass the outer horizontal tween into inner `ScrollTrigger` instances (`containerAnimation: horizontalTween`) to trigger headline masked reveals, counter roll-ups, image parallax, and **3D camera/artifact swaps** precisely as each panel crosses `left center`! *(Important GSAP constraint: nested `containerAnimation` triggers cannot pin or snap — put optional gentle `snap` only on the outer track trigger.)*
5. **Dual-Axis Input Intelligence:** On MacBook trackpads and Magic Mice, users intuitively try **both** vertical scroll and two-finger horizontal swipe once a section starts sliding sideways. Bridge Lenis `virtual-scroll` so horizontal `deltaX` gently contributes to vertical scroll progress while the horizontal reel is active!

#### Reference Implementation Pattern (React 19 + Next.js + GSAP `matchMedia`)
```ts
// Inside useEffect / useGSAP — strictly scoped with gsap.matchMedia()!
const mm = gsap.matchMedia();

mm.add("(min-width: 1024px) and (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)", () => {
  const track = trackRef.current!;
  const getScrollAmount = () => -(track.scrollWidth - window.innerWidth);

  const horizontalTween = gsap.to(track, {
    x: getScrollAmount,
    ease: "none", // Critical: preserves exact 1:1 scroll-to-position sync
    scrollTrigger: {
      trigger: stageRef.current,
      pin: true,
      scrub: 0.85, // Luxury weighted feel without sluggish lag
      start: "top top",
      end: () => `+=${Math.abs(getScrollAmount())}`,
      invalidateOnRefresh: true,
      anticipatePin: 1, // Prevents 1-frame jump when entering pin
      onUpdate: (self) => {
        // Feed mutable ref directly to R3F useFrame — zero React re-renders!
        cinemaScrollRef.current.horizontalProgress = self.progress;
      },
    },
  });

  // Nested per-panel storytelling triggered by horizontal position
  gsap.utils.toArray<HTMLElement>(".reel-panel").forEach((panel, index) => {
    gsap.fromTo(
      panel.querySelector(".panel-reveal"),
      { y: 36, opacity: 0 },
      {
        y: 0,
        opacity: 1,
        duration: 0.7,
        ease: "power3.out",
        scrollTrigger: {
          containerAnimation: horizontalTween,
          trigger: panel,
          start: "left 72%",
          toggleActions: "play none none reverse",
          onEnter: () => setActiveArtifactIndex(index),
          onEnterBack: () => setActiveArtifactIndex(index),
        },
      }
    );
  });
});

return () => mm.revert(); // Guaranteed cleanup on Next.js App Router navigation
```

### Pattern B: Sticky Pinned Multi-Slide Full-Screen Stage
> *Behavior:* The screen locks/sticks smoothly in place (`100svh` / `100vh`) while continued scrolling transitions **multiple full-screen slides or stacked layers** one after another — filling the viewport seamlessly — until the final slide finishes and vertical scroll resumes cleanly.

#### Three Luxury Visual Variants (Choose by Narrative Role)

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Architectural Curtain Wipe** *(Recommended Primary)* | Incoming slide enters via GPU-composited `clip-path: inset(100% 0% 0% 0%) → inset(0% 0% 0% 0%)` (or `yPercent: 100 → 0`) while outgoing slide subtly recedes (`scale: 1 → 0.95`, `opacity: 1 → 0.65`) | Feels like precision gallery walls gliding smoothly into place with tangible depth (*Figure-Ground*) | **Act II (4 Architectural Strata)** & **Case Study Hero → Problem → Solution** chapters |

| **3. #### Pacing & Timeline Architecture Secrets
- **Explicit Scroll Budget Per Slide:** Allocate **`75vh–95vh` of scroll distance per slide** (e.g., 4 slides = `end: "+=320%"` to `"+=360%"`). Too short (`30vh`) causes accidental skips; too long (`200vh` per slide) creates scroll fatigue and makes users feel stuck.
- **Timeline Labels + Dwell Buffers:** Give every slide a brief **readability plateau (dwell zone)** in the GSAP timeline (e.g., `0.45` transition + `0.35` calm hold) so the user can actually read the slide before the next wipe begins!
- **Optional Smart Directional Snap:** If snapping is enabled, use gentle, non-aggressive directional snapping (`snap: { snapTo: "labelsDirectional", duration: { min: 0.25, max: 0.55 }, delay: 0.12, ease: "power2.inOut" }`) — **never** fight active user scrolling or block fast scrolling past the section.
- **Synchronized 3D Camera Cuts:** Drive the persistent R3F `CameraRig` directly from the same scrubbed timeline progress (`0 → 1`) so every incoming slide aligns with a bespoke lighting angle and 3D assembly state.

#### Reference Pattern: Sticky Multi-Slide Curtain Wipe (GSAP Timeline)
```ts
const tl = gsap.timeline({
  scrollTrigger: {
    trigger: deckRef.current,
    pin: true,
    scrub: 0.75,
    start: "top top",
    end: () => `+=${(slides.length - 1) * window.innerHeight * 0.9}`,
    invalidateOnRefresh: true,
    anticipatePin: 1,
  },
});

slides.forEach((slide, i) => {
  if (i === 0) return; // Slide 0 is visible at start
  const prev = slides[i - 1];

  tl.addLabel(`slide-${i}`)
    // Incoming full-viewport slide wipes smoothly upward
    .fromTo(
      slide,
      { yPercent: 100, clipPath: "inset(0% 0% 0% 0% round 24px 24px 0px 0px)" },
      { yPercent: 0, clipPath: "inset(0% 0% 0% 0% round 0px 0px 0px 0px)", duration: 1, ease: "power2.inOut" }
    )
    // Outgoing slide gently recedes into depth (Figure-Ground clarity)
    .to(prev, { scale: 0.95, opacity: 0.55, duration: 1, ease: "power2.inOut" }, "<")
    // Subtle readability plateau before next slide starts
    .to({}, { duration: 0.35 });
});
```

### CSS Scroll-Driven Animations vs. GSAP ScrollTrigger (2026 Decision Framework)
In 2026, native CSS `animation-timeline: scroll()` and `view()` run off the main thread in Chrome, Edge, and Safari 26 (with Firefox still catching up behind flags), making a **hybrid strategy** the gold standard:

| Use Case | Recommended Engine | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pinned Horizontal Scroll Reel** | **GSAP ScrollTrigger (`pin` + `containerAnimation`)** | Native CSS Scroll-Driven Animations **deliberately do not support pinning** or cross-axis choreography |
| **Sticky Multi-Slide Timeline + 3D Sync** | **GSAP ScrollTrigger + Mutable Ref → R3F** | Requires explicit dwell plateaus, timeline labels, resize recalculation, and direct JS sync to WebGL camera uniforms |
| **Simple Progress Bars & Non-Pinned Image Parallax** | **Native CSS `animation-timeline: view()` / `scroll()`** *(wrapped in `@supports`)* | 0 KB JS, compositor-thread 60–120fps, zero main-thread contention |
| **React Route / Filter / Modal Springs** | **Framer Motion** | Declarative `AnimatePresence`, `layoutId` shared-element morphs, interruptible spring physics |

### Behavioral Psychology & Anti-"Scroll-Trap" Guardrails (*Laws of UX* Applied)
Pinned scroll mechanics can delight or infuriate depending on whether they respect human cognition. Enforce these **6 non-negotiable UX rules** grounded in the attached *Laws of UX (2026 Edition)* handbook:

1. **Law of Continuity (#30) — Always Show the Next Slide Peeking:** In horizontal reels, size primary panels to `78vw–86vw` (not blind `100vw`) so the edge of the upcoming project naturally bleeds onto the right side of the screen. Visitors immediately recognize horizontal flow without needing instructions.
2. **Goal-Gradient Effect (#48) & Zeigarnik Effect (#53) — Always Show Clear Progress:** Mount a sleek floating porcelain HUD strip inside every pinned section showing exact position (`02 / 04`), a continuous Neon-Teal progress line with a Neon-Orange active indicator, and clickable direct-jump pills so users know *exactly* how close they are to finishing the sequence.
3. **Mental Model (#19) & Jakob's Law (#39) — Zero Trapped Feeling:** Always include an unobtrusive **`Skip Section ↓`** button (and direct links in the Chapter Rail) so returning visitors or rushed recruiters can bypass any pinned reel in 1 click. Never hijack native browser `Back` navigation or alter normal scroll speed outside pinned ranges.
4. **Doherty Threshold (#37) — Keep Scrub Responsive:** Keep `scrub` between `0.5` and `0.9` (never `scrub: 2.5+`, which feels sluggish and unresponsive like wading through mud).
5. **Refresh Hygiene (`ScrollTrigger.refresh()`):** Call `ScrollTrigger.refresh()` after web fonts resolve (`document.fonts.ready`), dynamic MDX/images settle, and orientation changes finish (debounced `250ms`), and always create ScrollTriggers in **strict top-to-bottom DOM order** (or specify `refreshPriority`) so pin-spacer math never drifts!
6. **Lenis ↔ GSAP Single-Ticker Lock:** Initialize Lenis with `autoRaf: false`, bind `lenis.on('scroll', ScrollTrigger.update)`, drive `lenis.raf(time * 1000)` inside `gsap.ticker.add(...)`, and set `gsap.ticker.lagSmoothing(0)` so smooth scroll and pinned elements never experience 1-frame jitter.

## 11. Performance Budgets, Accessibility & QA Verification Gates

A luxury engineering portfolio proves seniority by **shipping cinema-grade visuals with SaaS-grade engineering discipline**.

### Hard Engineering Budgets (Release Gates)
| Metric | Target Budget | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LCP (Largest Contentful Paint)** | **≤ 1.5s** (Desktop), **≤ 2.2s** (Mobile 4G) | HTML typography & hero layout render immediately before 3D scene finishes warming up |
| **INP (Interaction to Next Paint)** | **≤ 100ms** (Strict Doherty Threshold compliance) | Keeps every hover, click, filter, and D-pad press feeling instantaneous |
| **CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift)** | **≤ 0.01** | Prevents luxury Swiss grids from ever jumping during font or 3D hydration |
| **Frame Rate (Desktop / Mobile)** | **60–120 FPS** (Desktop), **≥ 55 FPS** (Mid-range Mobile) | Enforced dynamically via R3F `PerformanceMonitor` tier downgrading |
| **Initial JS + 3D Critical Payload** | **< 220 KB gzipped JS** + **< 1.5 MB Draco GLB** | Fast global first visit; remaining Act models stream lazily |
| **WCAG 2.2 Contrast Compliance** | **≥ 4.5:1** body text, **≥ 3:1** large type & UI focus/controls | Guaranteed by Dual-Tier Neon vs. Accessible Ink token architecture |

### Multi-Device & UX Verification Checklist
1. **The 6-Second Recruiter Test:** Show Act I to a peer for 6 seconds — can they state your exact seniority, specialties, and click into a flagship case study effortlessly?
2. **Real-Device Matrix Audit:** Verify at `375×812` (iPhone SE/15), `768×1024` (iPad), `1440×900` (MacBook Pro), `2560×1440` (Desktop), and `3840×2160` (4K / 10-foot TV mode with keyboard arrows *only*, mouse unplugged).
3. **Zero-WebGL & Reduced-Motion Audit:** Disable WebGL and enable `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` — verify the site still reads like a luxury editorial Swiss SaaS flagship with zero blank holes.
4. **Scroll Continuity & Pin Integrity Audit:** Rapidly scrub up, down, resize the browser window, rotate mobile portrait↔landscape, and jump directly between chapters via the rail and browser Back/Forward buttons — verify zero stuck pins, zero iOS Safari `100vh` white gaps (`100svh` enforced), clean `ScrollTrigger.refresh()`, and seamless handoff between vertical scroll, horizontal reels, and sticky multi-slide decks.
